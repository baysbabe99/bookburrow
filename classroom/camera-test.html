If the camera will not open

  1. On an iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari itself, not from a home-screen icon.
  2. Tap Open the camera and then Allow when it asks.
  3. If it never asks: tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Camera → Allow, then reload.
  4. Still stuck? Send Mary the line marked Technical detail.
