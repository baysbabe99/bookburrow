Bookburrow Classroom

Classroom iPad Setup

About fifteen minutes with the teacher's iPad in your hands. One rule decides whether it works: the camera gets allowed in Safari before the app goes on the home screen.

0 of 10

Bring with you

  • The teacher's email address and a password they'll actually remember. No confirmation email will arrive — none is needed.
  • Their class roster as plain text, one name per line. First word becomes the first name, the rest the last name.
  • Five real books with barcodes, to scan in as a trial.
  • A decision on checkout length and book limit. Defaults are 14 days and 2 books; the teacher can change them later.
Steps 2 and 3 must come before step 9.

iOS will not give the camera to a page opened from a home-screen icon until it has been allowed once in Safari. Do it in the wrong order and the scanner is dead on an icon that looks perfectly fine.

On the iPad

If something's off

It reads, but makes no sound

Check the iPad's silent switch. iOS mutes Web Audio along with everything else, so a silenced iPad is a silent scanner. In the app, also check Setup → Confirmation sounds.

It won't read, but Frames looked at is climbing

The scanner is running fine — this is a distance problem, not a bug. Move closer, hold the book steady, and keep a shadow off the barcode.

There's a real ceiling here: past about eighteen inches it won't read at all. A foot away is the sweet spot.

Frames looked at is stuck at 0

The decoder never started. That's a genuine bug, not a technique problem. Send me the whole grey block — the Reader line especially, which says whether the iPad used its own barcode reader or the downloaded one.

"Camera trouble" in red

If it mentions the home screen, this iPad hit the ordering problem — open the link in Safari itself and allow the camera there.

Otherwise tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Camera → Allow, then reload.

Worth setting while you have it

Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to 5 minutes or more, so the iPad doesn't sleep in the middle of a checkout queue.

Leave them knowing

  • Books go out by scanning, then tapping the student's name. Students never log in to anything.
  • The Due tab has a red badge when something's late — that's the only thing worth checking regularly.
  • Nothing they do can lose a book. Deleting a shelf spot leaves the books; it just marks them unsorted.
  • If the scanner ever plays up, Type the ISBN instead is on every screen where the camera is.

Ticks are saved on this device only, kept separately for each teacher.
